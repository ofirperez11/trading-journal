# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Serious retail **futures day traders** — the primary user is Ofir, who scalps MNQ/MES on
Tradovate. Hebrew-first, non-technical about software but fluent in trading. Context of use:
reviewing the session after the close (or between trades) on a Mac, and quickly on iPhone.
The job: turn every logged trade into a clear, honest picture of performance — and improve.

## Product Purpose

A multi-journal trading journal (backtest + live + future client journals) that records trades
(manually, from a screenshot, or via Tradovate CSV) and renders **beautiful, rigorous analytics**.
Statistics are the backbone of the product. Success = the trader trusts the numbers at a glance and
finds the one insight that changes the next trade. Built multi-tenant/sale-ready on Supabase.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, confident — an **instrument**, not a marketing site. Voice is plain and direct
(active verbs, sentence case, no hype). Three words: *instrument, honest, disciplined*. The
interface should feel like a well-built trading terminal a professional trusts.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-SaaS with a violet→cyan gradient on glass (the 2026 AI default).
- Hype-y fintech marketing (navy + gold, "10x your trading").
- Loud gamified brokerage apps (confetti, dopamine reds/greens everywhere).
- Cream/serif/terracotta editorial templates.

## Design Principles

- **The data is the hero.** Numbers are rendered like terminal readouts; chrome stays quiet.
- **Green and red are earned.** Win/loss color is meaning, never decoration.
- **Clarity over cleverness.** A trader scanning fast should never misread a number or a direction.
- **One instrument, many journals.** The same vocabulary scales across journals and screens.
- **Honest empty/failure states.** Tell the trader what to do next, in the interface's voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. RTL Hebrew throughout. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on the graphite surface; never rely on
color alone for win/loss (pair with sign, arrow direction, and label). Visible amber focus rings,
full keyboard paths, and `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation.
